Common questions

How do I change my pay or budget?
Open Settings in the app and tap "Change pay and budget".

How do I delete my data?
Open Settings and tap "Delete all my data". Deleting the app removes everything too.

I got a new phone. Where is my data?
Penny keeps everything on your phone, so it moves only if you restore your new phone from a backup of the old one.

Is the take-home pay figure exact?
No. It is an estimate for planning. Your payslip is always the accurate figure.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Penny is a budgeting app made by Monty Nelson in the United Kingdom.

The short version: Penny does not collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What Penny stores, and where

Penny stores the following only on your device:

  • your pay details, payday and budget
  • the expenses you log, with any notes
  • receipt photos you choose to attach
  • your savings goals, streak, points and reminder settings

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Penny has no accounts, no sign-in and no connection to your bank. We cannot see your data and we could not hand it to anyone if asked.

What Penny does not do

  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No advertising inside the app.
  • No sharing or selling of data.
  • No third-party services receive your information.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used for reminders. They are scheduled by your phone itself; nothing is sent from a server.
  • Camera and photos: used only when you choose to attach a receipt photo to an expense. The photo is saved inside the app on your phone.

You can turn any of these off in your iPhone's Settings.

Deleting your data

Open Settings in the app and choose "Delete all my data" to remove everything, including receipt photos. Deleting the app also deletes everything.

Backups

If you use iCloud or computer backups, Apple may include the app's data in your own backup. That is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Children

Penny is not aimed at children under 13.

Not financial advice

Figures in Penny are estimates to help you plan. They are not financial, tax or investment advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If Penny ever starts collecting any data, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated first.
