Everything in the app

Budgeting that feels like a game.

Most budget apps make you feel guilty. Penny makes logging a quick daily habit, rewards you for showing up, and keeps everything private.

📅

Payday-to-payday budgets

Paid on the 25th? Your budget starts on the 25th. Monthly, weekly, fortnightly or four-weekly pay all work.

💷

Take-home estimate

Enter your salary before tax and Penny estimates UK take-home after Income Tax and National Insurance, with optional pension and student loan. Scotland included.

⚡

Log in seconds

Pick a category, type the amount, done. Your usuals sit one tap away. Attach a photo of a receipt if you like.

🧺

Needs and wants

Rent, bills, groceries, essentials and fun money, grouped so you can see what’s left in each part of your budget.

🙌

“I spent nothing today”

A no-spend day counts too. One tap checks you in and keeps your streak going.

🔔

Friendly reminders

Gentle nudges at a time you choose, with quiet hours and a payday heads-up. If you’ve already logged, Penny stays quiet.

🎯

Savings goals you can see grow

Name it (a weekend away, a new laptop, a rainy-day pot), set a target, and log each amount you put aside. Penny shows what’s left to go and celebrates when you get there.

Questions

Good to know

Can I download it yet?

Penny is in private iPhone beta right now. Follow us on TikTok or YouTube and we’ll post the App Store link the moment it’s live.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. You log expenses yourself, and Penny keeps your entries on your device.

Does it move money for me?

No. Move your money yourself, then log what you set aside so Penny can show your progress.

Does it work on Android?

We’re testing the iPhone version first.

Is the take-home figure exact?

It’s an estimate for planning, based on the current UK tax year. Your payslip is always the accurate figure.
